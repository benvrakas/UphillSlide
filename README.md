# Uphill Slide

Slide up steeper slopes. In vanilla Satisfactory a slide stops on anything steeper than about 7.4° uphill. This mod
lets you choose that limit for each Blade Runners tier, and how quickly a slide slows down going uphill.

## Settings

Open **Mods → Uphill Slide** (in game or in Satisfactory Mod Manager). Each tier has:

- **Steepest uphill slide (degrees)**: the steepest slope you can keep sliding up (0 to 89).
- **Uphill slowdown (%)**: how quickly the slide slows down going uphill. 100 is vanilla, 50 lasts twice as long,
  0 doesn't slow down uphill at all. Flat ground and downhill are unchanged.

| Tier | Default angle |
|---|---|
| Without Blade Runners | 7.4° (vanilla; enough for 1 m ramps) |
| Blade Runners | 15° (2 m ramps) |
| Mk.2 Blade Runners | 20° |
| Mk.3 Blade Runners | 27° (4 m ramps) |
| Mk.4 Blade Runners | 35° |
| Mk.5 Blade Runners | 40° |

Ramps on an 8 m foundation: 1 m is 7.1°, 2 m is 14.0°, 4 m is 26.6°.

## Mk+ Blade Runners (optional)

With [Mk+ Blade Runners](https://ficsit.app/mod/bbladerunners) installed, each Mk.2 to Mk.5 tier uses its own
settings, and the reduced and no fall damage versions count as their tier. Without it, those settings do nothing.
Blade Runners from other mods use the Blade Runners settings.

## Multiplayer

Everyone needs the mod. The host's settings (or the dedicated server's) apply to every player.
